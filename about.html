About Us

A simple tool for quick VAT calculations.

Who we are

Vat Calculator German is a free online tool that helps you add VAT to a net price in a few seconds. No sign-up, no downloads.

What we do

  • Add VAT to a net amount, or exclude VAT from a gross amount, with preset rates of 19%, 7% and 10%.
  • Use a custom rate whenever you need a different percentage.
  • See the net amount, VAT amount and gross amount clearly.

Our goal

Keep it fast, clean and easy to use for freelancers, shop owners, students and anyone who needs a quick VAT figure.

Results are for information only and do not replace professional tax advice.
