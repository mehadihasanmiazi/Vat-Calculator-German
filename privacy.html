Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Your privacy matters. Vat Calculator German is designed to collect as little information as possible.

2. Calculator data

All calculations happen in your browser. The amounts you enter are not sent to our servers and are not stored.

3. Cookies and local storage

We store your cookie choice (accepted or declined) in your browser so we do not ask you again on every visit. If we add analytics or advertising in the future, they will only be used according to your choice and this policy will be updated.

4. Contact form

When you use the contact form, your email app opens with your message. We only receive the details you choose to send us, and we use them only to reply to you.

5. Third parties

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

6. Your rights

You can clear your browser data at any time to remove the stored cookie choice. For any privacy question, please visit our Contact page.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
